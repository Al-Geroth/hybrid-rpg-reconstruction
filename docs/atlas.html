Mathematical atlas

A rating can describe a character, set the reach of a power, or supply the input to another rule. Those are different jobs. Follow one number through play before opening the full inventory.

How a number reaches the table

Imagine a molecular controller declaring that she will reshape an object. Rule 134 starts its example at 100.5 C2 DP. The player can divide life-span by five and multiply power by five. The resulting 502.5 C2 PL answers a question about the controller. The next step answers a question about the object. Copy the numeral 502.5 into C3 PL for the target capacity. Converting 502.5 through the scale equation would give a radically different answer.

The source has connected a player choice, a sheet update and a limit on a declared power. To use it for a new scene, the referee must already know the object’s size rating or decide how to assign it. The rule tells the group how much the controller may affect. It supplies no contest against another controller.

What the review added

The Phase I search marked 275 of the catalogue’s 304 segments. It linked 70 of those segments directly to 54 detailed mathematical records. We read the other 205 against the same v0.30 capture. All 275 candidates now have a disposition. The other 29 catalogue segments had no selected mathematical cue.

20 newly registered gameplay operations have explicit inputs, a stated fictional output and at least one numerical replay. A conditional procedure may still depend on a choice of branch. Copying a number to a different stat can be a new procedure even when the arithmetic is trivial. Additional examples, alternative coefficients, cross-game translations and setting calibrations have their own categories. The total is an editorial count of distinct operations, not a count of equals signs or a score for playability.

Families and their uses

Each family groups rules by the question a player or referee asks. Identical arithmetic can serve unrelated functions. Conversely, a single play decision may need several different kinds of arithmetic.

Magnitude and representation

How do I express the same capability on another scale?

Inputs
Numeral, magnitude, stat role and the intended direction.
Outputs
A numeral at the adjacent magnitude, retaining DP, PL or LS.
Domain
Positive input. Reverse conversion needs a branch. C0 requires a separate contextual assignment.
Gameplay connection
A converted C2 DP can feed AMBS; C3 converted back to C2 can feed a roll-under threshold.
Limit
Simply keeping the numeral and changing its class changes capability. Treating every reclassification as conversion corrupts the sheet.
Character construction and attributes

What initial sheet am I constructing?

Inputs
Campaign magnitude from the GM, a chosen or rolled DP, fictional character concept.
Outputs
A baseline, AMBS, a class and selected capabilities or traits.
Domain
Different starting methods are alternatives. Human, mutant and cosmic examples use different benchmarks.
Gameplay connection
Rule 123 carries an evolving C2 DP into its AMBS calculation. Rule 166 imports a technological size for a robot.
Limit
Several statistics are descriptive or imported. No universal starting budget, shop or exhaustive skill list follows.
Advantages and point allocation

What does assigning this advantage do to the rating?

Inputs
A starting rating and a sequence of fractions, or an existing block and a signed denominator.
Outputs
An increased rating under rule 123, or a specified portion of a block under rule 54.
Domain
The direction and notation in 54/138 have a different use from the successive fractions in 123.
Gameplay connection
The ninja example spends a share on telepathy; the low-powered mutant example gains named abilities as its baseline grows.
Limit
The names do not supply a general purchase tariff, nor does the share procedure fix a power's price.
Power and life-span exchange

What power can I obtain by shortening a life-span?

Inputs
A paired LS and PL and a specified operation or factor.
Outputs
New LS and PL numbers at the chosen magnitude.
Domain
The source gives additive, multiplicative and other paired operations.
Gameplay connection
The new PL can power molecular control in 134. A converted LS is used to estimate a duration in years.
Limit
The same reduction in LS can yield different PL after a different operation. A branch must be specified.
Life-span, waiting and persistence

How long does a life or a stated interval of rebirth last?

Inputs
A life-span rating, or C3 DP for the qualifying reincarnation case.
Outputs
Years of life or a wait before reincarnation.
Domain
The life-span multiplier changes at 20 C2. The reincarnation example has a limited fictional scope.
Gameplay connection
The lifespan estimate consumes LS from the exchange family. Reincarnation supplies a duration when its stated conditions hold.
Limit
There is no procedure here for wounds, recovery between scenes or buying a lasting increase in DP.
Travel and movement

Can the character run, fly or see far enough for the declared scene?

Inputs
A properly typed rating, or desired speed, mass, medium and sometimes a modifier.
Outputs
Speed, travel or observational range, or required movement rating.
Domain
The running, mutant movement, kaiju flight and astronomical travel rules have different inputs and domains.
Gameplay connection
One can compare a computed capability with a known distance or speed if the scene supplies that condition. The flight rule also enters a worked object-stat comparison.
Limit
A speed calculation alone does not say how much time passes in combat, how to dodge, or whether someone wins a chase.
Starship speed, size and range

Can an already described craft cover a route?

Inputs
Power, vessel size, a selected minimum cost, warp number, and in some rules a captain's DP.
Outputs
Warp number, speed multiple, journey range or estimated ship dimensions.
Domain
Warp-number calibrations and range equations vary. Length units must be fixed first.
Gameplay connection
Rule 10 produces a warp number. Rule 30 can turn it into speed and rule 130 uses warp in a separate range expression.
Limit
There are two speed branches even at the same warp number. Size-based price and transit time do not form a complete voyage procedure.
Size, density and construction

What dimensions or mass should I record for this creature or construction?

Inputs
Character or object rating, selected body model, and sometimes a damage die count, habitat or reference height.
Outputs
Height, length, number of storeys, density exponent or mass.
Domain
Different rules are for humans, mutants, giant creatures, technological objects or planets.
Gameplay connection
Rule 43 produces the exponent explicitly consumed by 44. Rule 15 derives height from a damage pool; 162 supplies a size for 166.
Limit
Even models sharing a unit can disagree. A measurement can describe a figure without deciding a conflict.
Strength, materials and endurance

How strong is a body or how resistant is an object?

Inputs
A C0/C1/C2 strength, a creature type or an assigned material.
Outputs
Strength rating, lifting weight or material benchmark.
Domain
Different formulas and units apply to different classes and the factorial branch has restricted inputs.
Gameplay connection
Rule 66 reuses rule 40 in a worked high-strength profile. Some object ratings are compared with external DEF/BOD.
Limit
The source mentions an endurance cost for shrinking but does not give a replenishing general endurance pool.
Special powers and affected targets

What size, number or scope can my power affect?

Inputs
An explicitly named power, its DP or PL role and its scale; sometimes baseline size, number of clones or apparent rating.
Outputs
Effect-specific extent, affected object class, passenger capacity, visible temperature rating or magnitude access.
Domain
The molecular control, clone, shrinking, thermal, phasing and teleportation models cannot exchange arbitrary outputs.
Gameplay connection
Rule 134 moves a number from the user's power to the target's size. Rule 19 uses a clone count to obtain a retained size fraction.
Limit
The extent describes what a power can do in a local model. An activation roll, duration, cost or contest may remain open.
Time travel and its cargo

What temporal distance or object can this traveller move?

Inputs
Rating and mode, sometimes portal population, dimensionality or target DP.
Outputs
Years or days of reach, portal size, or required traveller rating.
Domain
Astral travel, physical travel and an imitation made by clones have different meanings.
Gameplay connection
Rule 173 relates the target's C1 DP to the traveller's required C1 DP. The portal-size proposal in 94 reappears in 173.
Limit
A maximum number of years does not choose a timeline or determine the results of arrival.
Action and conflict

Can this declared action succeed?

Inputs
A chosen procedure, threshold, scale and a specified random result or comparator.
Outputs
A pass/fail reading, an estimated grade or a defensive value.
Domain
The 66 percent, two-thirds and roll-under readings are not a unified check. Rule 189 has several DCV alternatives.
Gameplay connection
Rule 188 converts a baseline into a roll-under target and compares a roll with it. A separate campaign decision may govern dice.
Limit
No general tested handoff from defensive value to a hit result, opposed roll or initiative order was found.
Damage and its consequences

What damage rating or dice pool does the attack have?

Inputs
An attacker's eligible C2 DP or C3 PL and a selected rule.
Outputs
A pool size, one-time maximum, or an object estimate called DEF/BOD.
Domain
Incompatible pool formulas and fractional results occur. DEF and BOD can be estimates for objects.
Gameplay connection
One-use N feeds a size calculation in 15. Some examples derive object statistics from a damage estimate.
Limit
The reviewed rules do not connect a successful roll and damage output to persistent personal injury, healing or death.
Setting and campaign calibrations

What number does the author assign to this fictional date, body, institution or occupation?

Inputs
External facts or GM assumptions such as a date, credits or population benchmark.
Outputs
A fictional rating, duration, income or size.
Domain
A scenario or particular fictional world supplies the benchmark and unit.
Gameplay connection
An academic credit load can feed the grade estimate. A galaxy's assigned baseline can enter a life-span trade.
Limit
A numerical salary or reign does not become a campaign economy, training system or advancement rule without further procedures.
Translation to other games

What other game's stat might this number resemble?

Inputs
A named source game, a named characteristic and a typed HYBRID rating.
Outputs
An approximate external stat or proposed HYBRID equivalent.
Domain
Each proposal is specific to the game's terminology and sometimes its edition.
Gameplay connection
Several passages repeat the CA-minus-ten SAGA comparison. A D&D hit-point number is compared with C3 DP.
Limit
An external numerical resemblance does not import the other game's combat, damage or recovery procedures.
Non-gameplay numerical material

Does this numerical mark help make a game decision?

Inputs
A date, a rhetorical example, a recipe quantity or an unsupported claim.
Outputs
Often a number, without a new game quantity or step.
Domain
Kept in the coverage audit so the search denominator is reproducible.
Gameplay connection
The recipe and masthead are examples of candidate search hits with no new gameplay equation.
Limit
These passages neither add to the new-operation count nor validate claims about the real world.

Where outputs go next

An explicit use means the source works or directs the transition. Compatible inputs could be connected by a reader; the cited example may simply choose the second number. A scene comparison also needs a condition supplied by the fiction. An endpoint is an output for which this review found no subsequent rule performing the indicated task.

Explicit use
  • The 45 C2 DP build ends with AMBS about 6.71. That is the square-root procedure of 49 applied to the new character. Rule 123 · b → Rule 49 · a
  • The roll-under example requires converting 35 C3 to C2 before subtracting ten and rounding. Accurate inversion and the author's printed approximation lead to different targets. Rule 45 · a → Rule 188 · b
  • Firestorm exchanges LS for PL, raising 100.5 to 502.5 C2 PL. The object affected is then rated 502.5 C3 PL. Rule 3 · a → Rule 134 · a
  • The White Lighter example doubles or quadruples power at the expense of life-span and then expresses that life-span in years. Rule 3 · a → Rule 141 · a
  • Ship length and C2 power determine warp. Rule 30 takes warp as its input, but two speed outputs survive. Rule 10 · a → Rule 30 · a
  • Density derived from C2 enters the power of height in the mass calculation. Passing C2 itself as the exponent would produce a different result. Rule 43 · a → Rule 44 · a
  • Rule 15 itself computes the one-use die count of 73, then divides C2 by its square root for height. The dice are used without an attack roll. Rule 73 · a → Rule 15 · a
  • The C2 size expression becomes the denominator when estimating the animal's C3-to-length factor. Rule 111 · a → Rule 113 · a
  • The robot procedure refers to the technology-length rule and explicitly changes a power rating into a baseline during construction. Rule 162 · a → Rule 166 · a
Compatible inputs
  • Rule 130 needs a warp number, so 10 could provide it. Rule 130's own Voyager example supplies warp ten directly. Rule 10 · a → Rule 130 · a
  • A known number of clones sets retained height by a reciprocal. Rule 19 also provides independent branches for a character with C1 PL or DP. Rule 18 · a → Rule 19 · a
Repeated operation
  • The second molecular-control passage repeats the same target-class mapping. The two citations are one operation. Rule 134 · a → Rule 152 · a
Scene comparison
  • A referee can compare a speed to a stated travel requirement. Rule 39 itself gives a speed-rating relation rather than a chase result. Rule 39 · a
  • Once a temporal cargo object has a C1 DP, its required traveller rating can be compared with a character sheet. A destination and timeline are additional inputs. Rule 173 · a
  • A passenger count can support a narrowly framed question about moving a known party. The leading device formula leaves fractional capacity unresolved. A separate group comparison discards the fraction in one example. Rule 174 · a
Unconsumed in conflict
  • DCV is produced. A HYBRID attack procedure consuming that value to decide a hit was not identified. Rule 189 · a
Unconsumed at injury
  • A pool of damage dice is produced. A general update to a living character's health after rolling that pool was not identified. Rule 146 · a
Descriptive endpoint
  • The builder's rating gives storeys and height. No procedure for paying for the building, completing it in time or using it to improve a character is supplied. Rule 92 · a
  • A cinematic vampire percentage has no identified downstream action, vulnerability or success calculation. Rule 51 · a
  • A lifetime cosmic appetite has no identified per-turn consumption interval, depletion track or recovery threshold. Rule 112 · a
  • The ring's recharge schedule is a property assigned to one fictional power source. The passage has no general fatigue-and-recovery procedure. Rule 117 · a
