Sources and method

The unit of evidence is a dated witness. The number on its cover is only one clue to its position in the publication history.

The copy behind this edition

The reference is the February 13, 2003 capture of Philippe Tromeur’s Wanadoo page. Its cover credits Matthew/C++. A February 11 capture on Tromeur’s Free.fr host has exactly the same 431,957 bytes and SHA-256 digest. The archive timestamp dates preservation. It cannot date every paragraph.

Read the reference witness in the Wayback Machine

What the versions change

The host linked v0.05, v0.10, v0.15 and v0.20. Two linked addresses replay Wanadoo error pages with HTTP 200. The numbered v0.05 file on Free.fr and its December 23, 2002 homepage recover the missing texts. The v0.15 header recalls having briefly used the label 0.3. That makes matching the host, header and capture especially important.

Rule 45 in v0.15 already walks from CA through C3. The December v0.20 page already has the optional pool in #17 and the roll-high wording in #35. The February v0.30 page also includes the contrary roll-under example in #188. By October 2007 the host labels the text v0.21, extends the chain in #45 to C4 there and adds a coin option to #35. Importing that coin toss would change the February game.

Archives, catalogues and reception

RPGGeek indexes uploads and version descriptions. Its file list was visible through the search index, while direct retrieval returned 403. RPGnet’s bibliographic entry discusses a later, much larger text. The Stack Exchange answer was readable through the web reader despite 403 from the byte downloader. It supplies the February link and reports a later blog. The blog homepage was retrievable, and its title identifies a later tri-stat version. None of those observations establish a February rule.

Editorial method

Record the author’s identifier and the position of the passage separately. Divide mixed passages at subject changes. Give each unit a primary category, secondary topics where applicable, a gameplay judgment, and an interpretation confidence. A mathematician can evaluate a formula with chosen inputs while a player still lacks the means to obtain those inputs. The scenario verdicts therefore answer a different question from the catalogue tags.

Segments are editorial boundaries. A different editor could reasonably split an example differently. Source order, heading counts and the checksum allow independent comparison. Notes preserve conflicting directions, exceptions and proposed interpretations. The mathematical registry concentrates on recurring, consequential or diagnostic constructions. The catalogue records the wider terrain.

Rights and redistribution

The disclaimer promises free reading on the host’s page, discusses a possible commercial hard copy, and acknowledges rights in third-party character material. We found no general grant to redistribute the complete text. The repository publishes new descriptions, mathematical notation, indexes and links. Source HTML, graphics from the original and derivative full-text dumps stay outside its release. Instrument Serif and Instrument Sans retain their accompanying SIL Open Font Licenses.
