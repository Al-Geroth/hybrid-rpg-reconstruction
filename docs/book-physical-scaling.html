Reconstructed rulebook · Chapter 7

Scaling from people to planets

Look for the unit at the end

Rule 39 explicitly measures running speed in mph. Rule 75 assigns a fictional galaxy a diameter in light-years from C1 DP. Rule 160 infers C2 DP from a diameter measured in metres. Each application needs its own physical unit and interpretation. Entering kilometres in an expression calibrated on metres changes the number inside the logarithm. Conversion of the physical unit and conversion along the C-scale are different operations.

The same algebra can serve different stories

Roots, logarithms, ratios and changes of scale recur. A ratio of two ratings can count copies in Rule 18. A square root gives AMBS in Rule 49. Rule 37 uses a proportional comparison with a standard height. These procedures can be evaluated independently. The author's program of connecting them through Psyche does not supply a mathematical derivation of every formula from the neighbouring-scale map.

How to read a claim about nature

Rule 136 grants extraordinary characters a threshold beyond which the author's version of physical law changes. This can define a property of an imagined world. Rule 4 assigns numerical modifiers to human groups and connects them to appearance. Neither a fictional exception nor a calculation with invented group coefficients establishes a scientific relationship. The catalogue records where such assumptions enter a mechanic.
