Reconstructed rulebook · Chapter 11

Where reconstruction runs out

Two routes to a movement rating

At 20 C2 DP, Rule 39 implies a running speed of 10 mph. Translating that rating by Rule 45 gives 49.28095 C3 DP. Rule 64 would read the latter number directly as mph for a mutant or metahuman. The text does not say whether the second route overrides the first for mutants running on foot. An unopposed ordinary runner and a mutant entering a race therefore pose different editorial problems.

Two meanings of a scale change

Rule 45 translates a rating while retaining its meaning. Rule 23 proposes turning a human into a super-mutant by keeping the written number and relabelling C3 DP as C1 DP. The second operation changes the character's magnitude. Treating both operations as a conversion that preserves strength would erase the power increase the second example is meant to achieve.

Which gaps matter in play?

The difference between roll-high and roll-under is visible and sometimes resolvable from the local example. A harder gap is the handoff from an individual success to an opponent's changed state, followed by the next turn. No cited calculation supplies that whole sequence. The seven playability trials track the last determinate step in each case so that a successful calculation receives credit even when the scene remains unfinished.
