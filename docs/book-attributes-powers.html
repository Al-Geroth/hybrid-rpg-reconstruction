Reconstructed rulebook · Chapter 4

Attributes, powers and effects

A derived score

Average Mind, Body and Soul, abbreviated AMBS, is the square root of C2 DP in Rule 49. At 36 C2 DP the result is 6. The rule gives an average. Inferring three separately determined attributes, each equal to 6 for every character at this rating, would throw away the distinction between a mean and its components.

B · Reconstructed reading

Source passages · #49 · #123

A power with a numerical effect

Rule 18 compares the creator's and the copy's C3 DP. Divide the first rating by the second and subtract one to obtain the number of additional copies. At a creator rating of 90 and a copy rating of 30, the result is two. These are trial inputs. Rule 128 presents other cloning expressions and calls attention to their differing results. Specify the rule and the ratings whenever using a copy count.

Source passages · #18 · #128

Possessing a power and using it

Rule 123 can establish that the character has camouflage. Rule 53 can compute a percentage for phasing or cloaking from a total and a base. The shared idea of concealment makes it tempting to treat the new character's entire DP as that total. Neither passage supplies that instruction for camouflage. A numerical rating of concealment and the result of an observer's attempt to detect the character therefore remain separate questions.

Source passages · #53 · #68 · #123

Temporary effects can alter later play

Rule 45 distinguishes a creator at DP from the created effect at PL. Its permanence example reassigns the creator's number to a different scale and allows restoration once the effect expires naturally. This gives the character a state that can change. The example leaves the timing of expiry open.
