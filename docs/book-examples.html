Reconstructed rulebook · Chapter 9

Three worked examples

Translate a courier's rating

Give a courier 16 C2 DP, the ordinary example in Rule 49. The square root gives AMBS 4. The scale map gives about 28.18 C3 DP. Nothing has happened to the courier. The two calculations answer different questions about one baseline.

Spend longevity on power

Rule 183 starts with a 40 C2 DP character, then halves the life-span rating to 20 C2 LS and doubles power to 80 C2 PL for a named effect. DP stays 40. Write the new labels beside the new numbers. Twenty here is a rating. Rule 6 provides a separate procedure for relating a life-span rating to a duration.

Source passages · #3 · #6 · #183

One die result, two verdicts

Rule 188 takes a state rated at 35 C3 DP, converts it to 17.7 C2 DP, subtracts 10 and rounds to an 8-or-less target. Using the increasing inverse of Rule 45 gives about 17.4826. Subtracting 10 and rounding to the nearest integer then gives 7 or less. A die total of 8 succeeds in the printed example and fails on the recalculated path. The source result and the editorial check are preserved side by side.
