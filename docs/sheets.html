Creating a character

To bring a character to the table, write down who they are, their starting Psyche with its class label, and one thing you want to try. Add a calculation only when a decision calls for it. Rule 47 supplies a compact humanoid profile. These two printable layouts are editorial reconstructions.

What belongs in each field?

“Required” below means needed for a typed rating in this worksheet. A source-wide list of mandatory fields has not been recovered.

Identity and concept

Required for a typed rating

Give the character a name and a short role. Rule 47 includes a name and age in its humanoid notation. A concept is this edition’s aid for play. #47

Campaign scale and starting DP

Required for a typed rating

Record the number, class, statistic and how the starting value was obtained. The GM selects a campaign magnitude. A benchmark and a rolled C1 baseline are different starting routes. #11 · #107

Age, occupation and source of a comparison

Optional

These establish who is acting. They do not grant a numerical skill, an initiative modifier or an experience award. #47

C2 baseline and AMBS

Derived when the inputs exist

Convert an equivalent rating when needed. The square root of C2 DP gives an average of Mind, Body and Soul. The three separate scores cannot be recovered from their average. #45 · #49

Working PL and LS

Derived when the inputs exist

Declare whether both start at default, then name the operation and its amount. The result of a trade depends on the operation chosen. #3 · #23 · #183

Lifespan in years

Derived when the inputs exist

Convert working C2 LS to C3, then apply the multiplier in rule 6. The reading below 20 C2 is interpretive and can yield more years than a higher rating. #6 · #31

Appearance allocation and remainder

Optional

Rule 47 sketches COM plus remaining power points. A worked case in rule 6 compares C2 PL and C2 COM. Supply both on a common scale before subtracting. #6 · #47

Advantages, effect, scope and limitations

Power-specific

Record each choice and the modified baseline. Add a range, duration, percentage or pool only when a passage gives one. A named limitation alone has no general refund price. #53 · #123 · #138 · #147 · #183

Action procedure and unit

Power-specific

Name a specific use, such as an unobstructed running speed in mph. Record the rule and which of its branches is being used. #35 · #39 · #64 · #188

Difficulty, injury, recovery and advancement

Unresolved unless a local rule supplies it

Leave room for a referee’s decision and identify its author. Borrowed health-point comparisons and permissions to take more advantages do not establish a general injury or reward schedule. #79 · #147 · #161
