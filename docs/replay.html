The session replayed

Jo and Ren play Kit and Double again. The belt still runs at 3 mph, Vale still wants the west platform served, and the same pair of bells still calls a single lift. Jo makes the same offer to trade life-span, and then to stay with the cuttings. This time the table has agreed on four additional rules before the GM opens the scene.

Where the starting numbers come from

Kit. Jo chooses the ordinary benchmark in Rule 49. Unmodified LS and PL start at the baseline for this example. No separate endurance, health or skill score is supplied. Rule 11 · Rule 49

Double. Ren combines the heroic C3 benchmark with the creator in Rule 18. The table grants Double that particular power and uses the appearance allocation assumed in its worked example. No general purchase price is inferred. Rule 18 · Rule 47 · Rule 182

Vale. The GM borrows the 18 C2 human example. Vale is competent, wants to keep the route west and will use the baseline rating without a trade. Rule 11 · Rule 35

Intentions fixed for both readings
  • Kit will walk the moving service belt, then try to switch the lift east. Jo is willing to shorten Kit's life-span rating for more power if a trade has that use.
  • Double will make one copy of 50 C3 DP. Double and the copy will ring the two call bells together.
  • Vale will hold the route west without trading life-span. If the lift goes east, Kit will stay to help the west crew through the night.

Who gets to be Double?

Before play, Ren puts the creator's 100 C3 DP on the sheet. This time the table has an explicit way to authorize the power.

GM “One lift. East has the moth keepers, west has Vale's trees. West is the current setting. When the lift leaves, its destination is settled for the night.”

What is on the sheet at dawn?

The east keepers travel with their moth cabinet. Kit and Vale take the cuttings inside by hand. The rescue has cost the west crew its ride and the courier a night of work. Neither cost needs to be translated into damage dice.

Ren “Once the car leaves, there's nothing left for either Double to ring. I erase the copy. What goes into next session?”

D · Referee decision or editorial addition

R2 closes the scene at the declared stake. R4 ends the single-scene duplicate. Double retains the starting baseline. No XP schedule is attached.

Rule 18 · Rule 45 · Rule 125 · Rule 128 · R2 · R4

GM “Write ‘east evacuated, west sheltered, Kit stayed’. Kit spends the night moving plants, so keep 8 LS and 32 PL through that work. At dawn a relief crew arrives. Kit takes the first uninterrupted quiet interval. Restore 16 LS and 16 PL then.”

D · Referee decision or editorial addition

R4 specifies exactly when the stored ratings return. The lift's destination and the work already done persist. Only the added recovery rule supplies this reset.

Rule 3 · Rule 6 · Rule 45 · Rule 183 · R4

What changed?

The same people end up at the same platforms in these two runs. In the source-led version, Vale's consent settles the route and Kit's proposed trade never changes the sheet. In the house version, Kit can force the selector east by taking a cost even if Vale continues to resist. Declining the trade would leave Kit at 16 against 18 and send the lift west. This counterfactual is the design change that matters. The copy's service, its expiry and Kit's recovery have also acquired explicit timing.
