Mathematical playground

Each instrument answers a question a player can ask at the table. Change values to explore its limits. Only the dice demonstration assumes fair, independent dice.

Equivalent scales

How would the same rating look elsewhere on the class ladder? Preserve DP, PL or LS while changing its numeral.

Positive inputs only. A backwards step from an output greater than one has two roots. C0 needs a local definition. #5 · #45 · #161

Power and lifespan

Starting at equal DP, PL and LS, spend lifespan to increase effective power. A fractional multiplier or a negative addition reverses the direction.

Keep the resulting ratings positive. The multiplier and the offset preserve different quantities. The final conversion to years uses an interpreted branch (B). #3 · #6 · #183

Running and mutant movement

Use the same supplied baseline with either movement passage. One route calibrates running on C2. The other reads a mutant’s numerical C3 rating as mph.

Use a positive rating. Comparing both results for one runner assumes that rule 64 covers running. Neither output answers an opposed chase. #39 · #64

Successive advantages

Follow one path through the low-mutant build. The demonstration chooses a named effect at each stage. The builder lets you change these choices.

The template illustrates four steps. Extending it to other character types or treating each step as an experience award requires another rule. #123 · #147

Fictional damage dice

Find the pool yielded by a specified game effect. The mutant calculation takes C2 DP. The two one-use alternatives take C3 PL. Changing the selector changes the meaning of the input numeral.

Use positive ratings. Fractional dice, injury, resistance, recovery and accumulated damage require decisions beyond these pool formulas. #73 · #118 · #146

Success thresholds

Compare two readings of the source’s high-roll language, or test the specific low-roll example. These procedures require different inputs.

Fair d6 probabilities support 1–40 dice in this reader. The denominator in the high-roll interpretation is C. Rule 188 gives an inclusive low target but does not specify a probability distribution here. #17 · #35 · #45 · #188
