exchange|Power and life-span exchange|What power can I obtain by shortening a life-span?|A paired LS and PL and a specified operation or factor.|New LS and PL numbers at the chosen magnitude.|The source gives additive, multiplicative and other paired operations.|The new PL can power molecular control in 134. life|Life-span, waiting and persistence|How long does a life or a stated interval of rebirth last?|A life-span rating, or C3 DP for the qualifying reincarnation case.|Years of life or a wait before reincarnation.|The life-span multiplier changes at 20 C2. space|Starship speed, size and range|Can an already described craft cover a route?|Power, vessel size, a selected minimum cost, warp number, and in some rules a captain's DP.|Warp number, speed multiple, journey range or estimated ship dimensions.|Warp-number calibrations and range equations vary. strength|Strength, materials and endurance|How strong is a body or how resistant is an object?|A C0/C1/C2 strength, a creature type or an assigned material.|Strength rating, lifting weight or material benchmark.|Different formulas and units apply to different classes and the factorial branch has restricted inputs.|Rule 66 reuses rule 40 in a worked high-strength profile. effects|Special powers and affected targets|What size, number or scope can my power affect?|An explicitly named power, its DP or PL role and its scale; sometimes baseline size, number of clones or apparent rating.|Effect-specific extent, affected object class, passenger capacity, visible temperature rating or magnitude access.|The molecular control, clone, shrinking, thermal, phasing and teleportation models cannot exchange arbitrary outputs.|Rule 134 moves a number from the user's power to the target's size. time|Time travel and its cargo|What temporal distance or object can this traveller move?|Rating and mode, sometimes portal population, dimensionality or target DP.|Years or days of reach, portal size, or required traveller rating.|Astral travel, physical travel and an imitation made by clones have different meanings.|Rule 173 relates the target's C1 DP to the traveller's required C1 DP. damage|Damage and its consequences|What damage rating or dice pool does the attack have?|An attacker's eligible C2 DP or C3 PL and a selected rule.|A pool size, one-time maximum, or an object estimate called DEF/BOD.|Incompatible pool formulas and fractional results occur.
